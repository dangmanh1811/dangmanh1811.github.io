👋 Hello there, I'm Manh!

👨‍💻 I'm a junior majoring in Artificial Intelligence at FPT University, Vietnam.

# Research Interests
My research interests lie at the intersection of Computer Vision and healthcare, with a particular emphasis on learning from imperfect and limited data. I focus on Domain Adaptation methods to enhance model generalization across heterogeneous medical datasets, addressing the challenges posed by distribution shifts in real-world clinical settings.

In addition, I am actively exploring Explainable AI techniques to improve the interpretability and trustworthiness of deep learning models in disease diagnosis. I am especially interested in developing systems that not only achieve high predictive performance but also provide meaningful explanations to support clinical decision-making.
